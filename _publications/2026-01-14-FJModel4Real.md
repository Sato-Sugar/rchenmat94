In this paper, we generalize the method of Gan-Ichino and Atobe to the field of real numbers and prove the basic tempered case of the local Gan-Gross-Prasad conjecture for Fourier-Jacobi models of symplectic-metaplectic groups, based on the tempered case of the conjecture for Bessel models proved by Chen-Luo.
